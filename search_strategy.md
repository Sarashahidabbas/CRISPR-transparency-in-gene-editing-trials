# Search strategy

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, data service version 2, CSV format.
Download date: November 2026 (the author will add the exact date).

## Search 1
Terms: CRISPR OR Cas9 OR "gene editing"
Result: 254 trials. File: data/raw/studies_search1.csv

## Search 2, wider terms
Terms: CRISPR OR Cas9 OR Cas12 OR Cas13 OR "gene editing" OR "genome editing" OR "base editing" OR "prime editing" OR TALEN OR "zinc finger nuclease" OR "gene-edited"
Result: 314 trials, which include all 254 from search 1. File: data/raw/studies_search2_wide.csv

## Merge
Duplicates were removed by NCT number. The merged set has 314 unique trials, 60 of them found only by search 2.

## Known limits
The search finds trials that use these words in their registry record. Trials that only use a product code may be missed. Registry records can be incomplete or out of date.
