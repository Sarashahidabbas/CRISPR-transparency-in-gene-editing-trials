# Coding protocol

Each trial is read on its ClinicalTrials.gov page. Use the link in the Study URL column.

## Step 1. Final category
Check every Medium and Low confidence trial in docs/review_queue.csv. Use one of these categories.

- Therapeutic gene editing: participants receive an edited cell product or a gene editing drug.
- Follow-up or non-interventional (gene editing linked): long term follow up or screening linked to a gene editing product.
- CRISPR used in diagnostics: CRISPR is used in a test.
- Research use or unclear: CRISPR or editing is only used in the laboratory, or the record is not clear.
- Not gene editing or unclear: no gene editing in the study.

## Step 2. Ethics and consent items, for therapeutic and follow-up trials
- Germline or somatic editing: Germline, Somatic, Not stated
- In vivo or ex vivo: In vivo, Ex vivo, Both, Not stated
- Consent mentioned in the record: Yes, No, Not stated
- Ethics approval mentioned in the record: Yes, No, Not stated
- Long term follow up mentioned in the record: Yes, No, Not stated

Code only what the registry record says. If it is not written, code Not stated. Do not guess.

## Step 3. Second coder
A second person codes 20 randomly chosen trials without seeing the first answers. Agreement is reported with percent agreement and Cohen kappa.
